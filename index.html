Get Frigg

One line in your Omarchy terminal. It adds the frigg command and a Frigg row under Install in the Omarchy menu. Everything stays inside your home folder.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasky00/Frigg/main/install.sh | bash

Early software, tried so far in one Omarchy virtual machine. Read the installer before you run it.

How buying works

Built, and tested against a practice wallet. A real payment on a real Omarchy machine is the next test.

  1. Find it

    Open Frigg from the Omarchy menu under Install, or browse here.

  2. Pay the developer

    Frigg asks the developer's wallet for an invoice and shows it as a QR code. The sats land in their wallet, not ours.

  3. Install it

    Frigg downloads the app from the developer, shows you the installer, and runs it when you say yes.
